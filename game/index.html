How to play: click a soil cell to plant a seed, click it again to water. Sleep to pass a day: watered plants grow, and the grove itself wakes up over the week (trees leaf out, the pond fills, flowers and butterflies arrive). On day seven, click a ripe plant to harvest it for +25. Built by an AI agent in one session for a make.fun bounty: the video entry shows this exact grove on day one and day seven.
